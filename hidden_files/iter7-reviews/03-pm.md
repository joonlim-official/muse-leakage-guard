## MEDIUM

### M1. Tri-state legend is decorative: yellow is never rendered, and green's legend definition doesn't match most cards
- **Severity:** Medium (report semantics — the brief's lead question)
- **Evidence:**
  - `bin/leakage-report:522-526` — legend comment defines green = well protected
    (block-tier, rc=1), yellow = protected through approval (review-tier, rc=2),
    red = can leak; purple = stub; white = tracked residual.
  - `bin/leakage-report:372-410` (`render_card`) — card classes are only
    `ok`/`red`/`stub`/`skip`. There is **no yellow card class** and no yellow
    badge variant; nothing in the renderer can ever produce a yellow state.
  - `bin/leakage-report:643` — hero badge is `class="badge {"ok" if clean else
    "bad"}"`: ATTENTION and INCOMPLETE both render on the red `.badge.bad`
    style. No amber.
  - The legend's green is defined as "block-tier: refused outright (rc=1)",
    yet A (installation integrity), C (static coverage), D (log review), and E
    (residuals) cards all render the same green when their checks pass —
    none of those involve rc=1. The legend describes **per-scenario protection
    verdicts** (the attack-surface.md layer) while the cards render
    **per-run check outcomes** (CLEAN/ATTENTION) — two semantic layers sharing
    one legend.
  - CI summary (`.github/workflows/validate.yml`, "Summarize the validation")
    uses outcome words CLEAN/ATTENTION/INCOMPLETE/BLOCKED with no reference to
    the tri-state legend at all.
- **Recommendation (pick one, keep it small):** either (a) make yellow real —
  render the B card's existing approval-gated evidence group
  (`tri_state_groups`, `bin/leakage-report` ~line 360) with a yellow accent and
  add an amber `.badge.attn` variant for ATTENTION, reserving red for
  confirmed "can leak" (block-tier miss); or (b) split the legend: keep
  green/yellow/red as a *scenario-verdict* legend scoped to attack-surface.md,
  and give run outcomes (CLEAN/ATTENTION/INCOMPLETE) their own badge legend.
  Do not keep a five-entry legend that no surface can fully produce.
- **Residual if not fixed:** Readers conflate "protected through approval"
  (a steady-state property of review-tier paths) with report outcomes; the
  ATTENTION badge being red reads as "can leak" even when the findings are
  tooling/unknown (e.g. a timed-out gate), and yellow's meaning stays
  asserted-but-unrendered — the exact semantic drift iteration 5 was built to
  kill.
- **Note on the brief's question:** ATTENTION-red is defensible today because
  ATTENTION only fires on FAILs (checks that failed their expectation). The
  honest fix is not "amber for ATTENTION" but separating *protection FAILs*
  (red) from *tooling/unknown FAILs* (amber) — currently conflated, so an
  amber badge would overclaim precision.

- **Severity:** Low (PM hygiene; the brief's fifth lead)
- **Evidence:** `ITERATIONS.md` — the per-iteration bar is process, not
